Why This Pack Looks Like This
You want work booked in. We think we can help — and not by starting on Monday

⭐ You are the straightforward one. The other businesses we looked at this week told us they could not resource the work they already had. You have thin books and want them full, which is a problem advertising is actually built for.

⛔ But there are two reasons we would not switch a campaign on this week, and both are in this pack. One is that your website would buy you the wrong customers. The other is that it is October.

There is no pricing in any of these documents.

Everything In One Place
  • Pre-Call Audit — start here. The two findings that matter, what is genuinely good, six further problems and seven questions.
  • Positioning — the two businesses described on your website, the exact lines costing you money, and what we would change against what we would leave alone. ⭐ This is the most important document in the pack.
  • Plan of Action — four phases from October to May, the winter lane, and the three things that would make us tear the plan up.
The Short Version
⭐ What is already good

⭐⭐ Booked solid for six months in your first year on nothing but word of mouth. That is the most impressive fact in this pack — it means the work is good and people recommend you, which is the hard part and cannot be bought. A £25,000 job inside year one. A Completed Jobs page, a Customer Reviews page, a working contact form, a landline as well as a mobile, and a wide construction service list.

⭐ And you have never advertised, so nothing is wasted and there are no bad habits to undo.

🔴 1. Your website is selling garden maintenance. Your money is in construction

Your homepage offers “comprehensive garden maintenance”, says “regardless of size or budget” twice, and your email is keetonsgardens@gmail.com. Your biggest recent job was £25,000.

🔴 Advertising produces more of whatever you are already asking for. So we would fill your diary with fence repairs and tidy-ups, you would be busy, and you would be poorer. ⭐ Your books would fill. That is the danger, not the reassurance.

⚠️ 2. Your books may be thin because it is October, not because anything is wrong

You were booked out for six months — roughly April to September — and went quiet in October. ⚠️ That is the shape of nearly every landscaping business. If it is seasonal, advertising hard in November buys the least interested audience of the year at full price. You have traded one season, so neither of us can know yet, and it is the first thing to establish.

🔴 3. No measurement, and neither phone number is clickable

No analytics, no tag manager, no pixel. ⛔ Not optional if you are about to spend money. And there is not one tap-to-call link on the site — most people will see it on a phone and have to copy a number out by hand.

🔴 4. Nothing about insurance, guarantees or accreditation

Set that against a £25,000 job and a company incorporated eleven months ago. ⭐ A referred customer does not need it because somebody vouched for you. A stranger from an advert needs it badly.

The Question That Decides Everything Else
What is an average job worth — not the biggest, the typical one? ⭐ If it is several thousand pounds, you have a genuinely good business to advertise and we should get the foundations right now and spend properly in February. If it is a few hundred, we would tell you to fix the words on your website first and we would sell you nothing on Monday. Everything else in this pack hangs off that one number.
